Fuel Oil Analysis

Fuel quality plays a critical role in ensuring safe and efficient engine performance. Regular fuel analysis helps in identifying contamination, degradation, or non-compliance with international fuel standards (such as ISO 8217 2017 ). OBW Testing offers precise and reliable testing for marine fuels, diesel, and other petroleum products to ensure compliance, prevent equipment damage, and maintain operational efficiency.

1 Viscosity at 50 °C (mm²/s) ISO 3104 / ASTM D445
